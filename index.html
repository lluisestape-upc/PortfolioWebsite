About
Background

I'm a Telecommunications Engineering student at UPC TelecomBCN in Barcelona, with a focus on Audio and Video Signal Processing. I design and build VST3 plugins from scratch in C++/JUCE, write DSP tools in MATLAB, and research ML systems combining graph neural networks with quantum circuits. I also build gesture-controlled instruments with computer vision, and embedded & robotics systems — from a 3-MCU ESP32 audio handheld to an autonomous warehouse drone running ROS2 and LiDAR SLAM.

Beyond engineering, I work as a Cybersecurity Strategy Consultant at Deloitte, have worked in film production at Arcadia Motion Pictures and Mundo Cero, and produce music independently on BeatStars.

I'm fluent in Spanish, Catalan, and English, conversational in French. Handball player of 10+ years.
